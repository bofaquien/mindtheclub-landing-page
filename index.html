Why Choose MindTheClub?

Experience private, peer to peer communication that puts you in control.

Direct Connection

Connect directly with the people who matter, no logins, no authentication, no e-mail and no phone number sharing required.

True Peer-to-Peer Messaging

Enjoy peer-to-peer messaging, that's stored exclusively on your device and your contact’s device.

Uncompromised Security

Every message is secured with full end-to-end encryption and travels directly, with nothing in between. One-to-one or in a group, the protection is identical, there is no weaker tier for group chats.

Offline Bluetooth Connection

When two people are within range, MindTheClub connects them directly over Bluetooth, no internet or Wi-Fi needed. Stay in touch even when the network can’t reach you.

Everything you'd expect from a messenger

Going peer-to-peer doesn't mean going without. MindTheClub delivers the full messaging experience you're used to, it just does it without a server in the middle.

Full Expression, No Compromise

Photos, videos, voice messages, GIFs, stickers, emoji reactions, they all travel the same way your text does: directly from your device to your contact's, through an encrypted channel.

Big Files, Same Principles

Need to send a document or a long video? MindTheClub breaks it into chunks and transfers them over the peer-to-peer connection.

Crystal Clear Audio and Video Calls, Total Privacy

Beyond messaging, connect face-to-face or voice-to-voice with unparalleled clarity and security.

Direct & Private Conversations

Experience high-fidelity audio and video calls that connect you directly with your contact, bypassing servers for ultimate privacy. Group video calls are available too.

End-to-end Encryption

Every call is secured with robust end-to-end encryption, ensuring your personal conversations remain yours and only yours.

Exceptional Quality, Always

Powered by WebRTC technology, enjoy stable, high-definition calls whether you're catching up with friends or collaborating with colleagues.
