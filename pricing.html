One price. Nothing to sell.

Free for 30 days, then less than a coffee a year buys you a messenger with no company in the middle.

Your first 30 days are free

MindTheClub is free to use for 30 days. The trial starts when you activate the app, not when you download it, so you do not lose days to an install you have not opened yet.

Nothing is charged during the trial and you are not asked for a payment method to begin. If you decide MindTheClub is not for you, do nothing and it simply stops.

After the trial

$1.39 a month

Prices are set per country and include local VAT where it applies. Google Play charges in your own currency and shows you the exact amount before you confirm.

Shown in US dollars. Your own country’s price and currency appear on Google Play before you confirm.

Base price: EUR 1.20 a month. Google Play sets the final amount for each country, converting the currency and adding local tax where it applies.

Why there is a price at all

MindTheClub has no servers holding your messages, which means there is no pile of data to mine, no profile to build and nothing to sell to anybody. That is the whole point of the design, and it is also why the usual way of paying for a messenger is closed to us.

So the subscription is the entire business model. You are the customer, not the inventory. No advertising, no trackers, no analytics on your behaviour, no selling anything to anyone, ever.

What the subscription covers

  • Unlimited direct peer-to-peer messaging, calls, photos, video and files.
  • Group chats and group video calls.
  • Encrypted backup and restore.
  • Every feature in the app. There is no higher tier and nothing is held back.

Cancelling

The subscription is managed by Google Play, so you cancel it there, in Play’s subscription settings, at any time. It runs until the end of the period you have paid for and does not renew after that.
