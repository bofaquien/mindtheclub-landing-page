Who pays for MindTheClub? You do, and that’s the point.

Every messenger costs money to run. When you don’t pay for it, someone else does, and they always want something back:

  • Free, because you are the product. Your contacts, your habits and your metadata become the business.
  • Free, because something bigger is planned. Some apps are the shop window for a crypto platform or a future business, and they follow that plan, not yours.
  • Free, thanks to grants or donors. Honest, but fragile: when the money stops, the app slows down or disappears.

MindTheClub works the other way round. Your subscription is our only income: no investors, no advertisers, no data to sell, nobody else to please. That makes the app self-sustaining and predictable. It grows with the people who use it, and it won’t vanish because a sponsor changed their mind.

You pay a fair price, and you keep your privacy. That’s the whole deal.

  • Every feature. No premium tier, nothing held back.
