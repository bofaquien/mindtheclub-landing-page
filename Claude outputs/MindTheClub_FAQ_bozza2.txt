LA QUESTIONE SFU, PERCHE' MERITA DUE MINUTI

SFU sta per Selective Forwarding Unit. In parole semplici e' un server che
riceve il flusso video di ogni partecipante e lo ridistribuisce agli altri,
invece di far collegare ognuno con tutti. Si usa perche' in un gruppo da
sei persone il collegamento diretto di ognuno con ognuno sono trenta flussi
video in uscita da ogni telefono, e un telefono non regge.

Il tuo commento su D28 e' la cosa piu' utile che mi hai scritto oggi,
perche' mi ha fatto trovare una contraddizione che era gia' sul sito.

Il problema. La tua homepage, nella sezione "Uncompromised Security", dice:
"One-to-one or in a group, the protection is identical, there is no weaker
tier for group chats". Per i messaggi di gruppo e' vero, viaggiano con il
protocollo gossip da dispositivo a dispositivo. Per le videochiamate di
gruppo, con un SFU in mezzo, non e' piu' vero allo stesso modo, perche' il
flusso non e' piu' diretto.

Questo e' esattamente il tipo di frase che ti si ritorce contro. Basta un
utente tecnico che apra un thread su Reddit intitolato "MindTheClub dice
peer-to-peer ma le chiamate di gruppo passano da un server" e quel thread
diventa la cosa che i sistemi AI citeranno di te per anni. Il rimedio costa
una riga, e dichiararlo tu per primo ti fa guadagnare credibilita' invece
di perderla.

Quindi ho fatto tre cose:
  - D28 dichiara l'SFU apertamente
  - D16 non dice piu' che le chiamate di gruppo sono identiche, distingue
    fra messaggi di gruppo e chiamate di gruppo
  - D20, la domanda sui limiti, elenca l'SFU fra i limiti

E ti chiedo una cosa sola, che io non posso sapere guardando il sito.

  (in WebRTC si fa con le insertable streams, cioe' i frame restano cifrati

Ho preparato in D28 le due versioni gia' scritte, VARIANTE A e VARIANTE B.

SEZIONE 1 - THE BASICS


D01 | Sezione: The basics
DOMANDA:
Is there a messaging app that does not require a phone number?

RISPOSTA:
Yes. MindTheClub works without a phone number, an email address, a
username or a password. You add a contact by scanning their QR code in
person or by tapping an invite link they send you. Nothing about your real
identity is needed to register, and nothing is verified against a phone
network.


D02 | Sezione: The basics
DOMANDA:
What is MindTheClub?

RISPOSTA:
MindTheClub is a peer-to-peer encrypted messaging app for Android. Text,
photos, files, voice messages and audio and video calls travel directly
from one device to the other, without a company server in between holding
the content. It is open source under GPLv3 and costs EUR 1.20 a month after
a 30-day free trial.


D03 | Sezione: The basics
DOMANDA:
What is a peer-to-peer messaging app?

RISPOSTA:
A peer-to-peer messaging app sends your message straight from your device
to your contact's device, instead of uploading it to a company server that
then passes it on. The practical difference is that no third party ever
holds a copy of your conversation, not even an encrypted one waiting to be
collected.


D04 | Sezione: The basics
DOMANDA:
Does MindTheClub have servers?

RISPOSTA:
MindTheClub runs no server that stores or relays message content. Three
small supporting services exist: a Cloudflare Worker that helps two devices
find each other, a Firebase directory holding a random user ID, a push
token and a public key, and Firebase Cloud Messaging to wake a sleeping
phone. None of them can see what you write.


D05 | Sezione: The basics
DOMANDA:
Can MindTheClub read my messages?

RISPOSTA:
No, and not because of a promise. Message content never reaches
MindTheClub's infrastructure at all, so there is no inbox, no database and
no encrypted archive on the company side to read, to hand over or to lose
in a breach. Messages exist only on your device and your contact's device.


D06 | Sezione: The basics
DOMANDA:
Is MindTheClub open source?

RISPOSTA:
Yes. MindTheClub is released under the GPLv3 licence and the full source
code is published on GitHub at github.com/AndreaVagheggi/mindtheclub. You
can read the code, check the claims on this page against it, and build the
app yourself if you prefer not to trust the version on Google Play.


D07 | Sezione: The basics
DOMANDA:
Who makes MindTheClub?

RISPOSTA:
MindTheClub is developed by MindTheClub LTD, a company registered in
England and Wales under company number 17184156. It is funded entirely by
its subscription. There are no advertisers, no investors buying access to
user data, and no third party paying for anything inside the app.


SEZIONE 2 - HOW IT WORKS


D08 | Sezione: How it works
DOMANDA:
How does MindTheClub send a message without a server?

RISPOSTA:
MindTheClub opens a direct encrypted channel between the two phones using
WebRTC, the same open standard that carries video calls in web browsers.
Your message enters that channel on your device and leaves it on your
contact's device. Servers are involved only in setting the channel up, not
in carrying what you send.


D09 | Sezione: How it works
DOMANDA:
What is WebRTC and why does MindTheClub use it?

RISPOSTA:
WebRTC is an open standard, built into browsers and phones, that lets two
devices open a direct encrypted connection to each other. MindTheClub uses
it because it is the mature, widely reviewed way to move data device to
device. Encryption in transit is DTLS, the standard WebRTC encryption,
rather than something invented for this app.


D10 | Sezione: How it works
DOMANDA:
Why does MindTheClub use Firebase Cloud Messaging if it has no servers?

RISPOSTA:
Because Android suspends network connections on a sleeping phone, and
Firebase Cloud Messaging is the mechanism Android provides to wake an app.
The wake-up carries no message content, only a signal to connect. The
sender's identity is encrypted inside the payload, so the push service
cannot see who is contacting you.


D11 | Sezione: How it works
DOMANDA:
What is a TURN relay and can it see my messages?

RISPOSTA:
A TURN relay is a fallback used when two devices cannot reach each other
directly, usually because of a restrictive mobile network or firewall.
MindTheClub uses Cloudflare's TURN service. The relay forwards encrypted
packets and cannot read them. It is comparable to a courier carrying a
sealed envelope: the destination is visible, the contents are not.


D12 | Sezione: How it works
DOMANDA:
What is signalling and what can the signalling server see?

RISPOSTA:
Signalling is the short exchange two devices need before a direct
connection can open, covering network addresses and connection parameters.
MindTheClub carries it over a Cloudflare Worker that keeps nothing on disk.
The payload is encrypted with the recipient's public key, and the two
parties appear as opaque per-session identifiers that do not repeat.


D13 | Sezione: How it works
DOMANDA:
How does MindTheClub verify that I am talking to the right person?

RISPOSTA:
Your QR code and your invite link both carry a fingerprint of your public
key. When someone scans or taps, their device fetches your key, computes
the fingerprint itself and rejects the key outright if it does not match.
Their own fingerprint then comes back sealed with your verified key, so
both sides are checked after one scan.


D14 | Sezione: How it works
DOMANDA:
What happens if the other person's phone is off or offline?

RISPOSTA:
The message waits on your own device and is delivered later, automatically.
MindTheClub uses Android's WorkManager to keep retrying across app
restarts, reboots and battery optimisation. Nothing is parked on a server
in the meantime, which is why delivery is not instant when the recipient is
unreachable, but it does complete.


D15 | Sezione: How it works
DOMANDA:
How do group messages work without a server?

RISPOSTA:
MindTheClub uses a gossip protocol. The sender delivers the message to the
group members currently reachable, and those members pass it on to members
the sender could not reach. A member who comes online later and notices a
gap requests the missing message from anyone who has it, not only from the
original sender.


D16 | Sezione: How it works
DOMANDA:
Are group chats less secure than one-to-one chats in MindTheClub?

RISPOSTA:
No, with one exception worth stating plainly. Group messages, photos and
files use the same end-to-end encryption and the same direct
device-to-device delivery as one-to-one chats, with no weaker tier and no
server-side group inbox. Group video calls are the exception: they pass
through a media server rather than travelling directly between devices.


SEZIONE 3 - COMPARISONS


D17 | Sezione: Comparisons
DOMANDA:
How is MindTheClub different from Signal?

RISPOSTA:
Signal is end-to-end encrypted but routes messages through Signal's own
servers and, in most countries, still asks for a phone number.
MindTheClub asks for no phone number and routes message content nowhere:
it goes device to device. Signal is far larger, more mature and
independently audited, which MindTheClub is not yet.


D18 | Sezione: Comparisons
DOMANDA:
How is MindTheClub different from WhatsApp?

RISPOSTA:
WhatsApp encrypts message content but requires a phone number, keeps
account and connection metadata, and belongs to an advertising company.
MindTheClub requires no phone number, holds only a random ID, a push token
and a public key, and is paid for by its users. WhatsApp is free and has
billions of users; MindTheClub is a paid app with a small user base.


D19 | Sezione: Comparisons
DOMANDA:
How is MindTheClub different from Session, SimpleX or Briar?

RISPOSTA:
All four avoid phone numbers, but they route differently. Session passes
messages through a network of service nodes, SimpleX through relay servers,
and Briar connects over Tor or local Bluetooth with no push service.
MindTheClub opens a direct WebRTC channel between the two phones and uses a
push wake-up so messages arrive without the app running.


D20 | Sezione: Comparisons
DOMANDA:
What are the limitations of MindTheClub?

RISPOSTA:
MindTheClub is Android only today. A desktop version is in development, and
there is no iPhone or web version. It has had no independent third-party
security audit. The user base is small, so most of your contacts will not
already be on it. Group video calls pass through a media server rather than
device to device.


SEZIONE 4 - PRIVACY AND DATA


D21 | Sezione: Privacy and data
DOMANDA:
What data does MindTheClub collect?

RISPOSTA:
For each user MindTheClub holds three things in its routing directory: a
randomly generated user identifier, a push notification token and a public
key. No name, no email address, no phone number, no contact list, no
message content. IP addresses pass transiently through Cloudflare while a
connection is being set up and are not retained by MindTheClub.


D22 | Sezione: Privacy and data
DOMANDA:
Does MindTheClub know who I talk to?

RISPOSTA:
No. There is no server-side record of who messages whom. Signalling
identifiers are derived from a random per-session channel ID, so they
change every session and cannot be linked across sessions. The push
wake-up uses a sealed-sender design, meaning the sender's identity stays
encrypted and is not visible to the push service.


D23 | Sezione: Privacy and data
DOMANDA:
Does MindTheClub need access to my contacts, my email or my name?

RISPOSTA:
No. MindTheClub never asks for your name or email address, and it works
without touching your address book. One optional feature does ask
permission to read your contacts: it lets you pick someone and send them
your MindTheClub profile by MMS. That list stays on your phone and is never
uploaded anywhere.


D24 | Sezione: Privacy and data
DOMANDA:
Can anyone see my IP address when I use MindTheClub?

RISPOSTA:
Your IP address is visible to Cloudflare while a connection is being set
up, and to the person you are connecting to, because a direct connection
requires each side to know where to send packets. This is inherent to
peer-to-peer. Running a third-party VPN on your phone hides it from both.
MindTheClub does not include a VPN of its own.


D25 | Sezione: Privacy and data
DOMANDA:
Where are my messages stored, and what happens if I lose my phone?

RISPOSTA:
Your messages are stored only on your device and your contact's device.
There is no cloud copy, so if you lose the phone and have no backup, the
conversations are gone. MindTheClub offers an encrypted backup, using
AES-256-GCM, that you create and control yourself.


D26 | Sezione: Privacy and data
DOMANDA:
Does MindTheClub show ads or use trackers?

RISPOSTA:
No. MindTheClub carries no advertising, no analytics on your behaviour and
no third-party tracking code. The subscription is the entire business
model, which is stated plainly because a peer-to-peer design produces no
data to sell even if the company wanted to.


SEZIONE 5 - FEATURES


D27 | Sezione: Features
DOMANDA:
Does MindTheClub work without an internet connection?

RISPOSTA:
Yes, over Bluetooth. When two people are within Bluetooth range,
MindTheClub connects their devices directly, with no internet and no Wi-Fi
needed. Outside Bluetooth range an internet connection is required on both
devices, because the message travels directly between the two phones rather
than waiting on a server.


D28 | Sezione: Features
DOMANDA:
Can I make video calls on MindTheClub?

RISPOSTA:

Yes. One-to-one audio and video calls are end-to-end encrypted and travel
over the same direct peer-to-peer connection as your messages. Group video
calls work differently: they are routed through a media server, an SFU
hosted on Cloudflare, because connecting every participant directly to
every other does not scale. Group calls are therefore not peer-to-peer.

Yes. One-to-one audio and video calls are end-to-end encrypted and travel
over the same direct peer-to-peer connection as your messages. Group video
calls are routed through a media server, an SFU hosted on Cloudflare,
because connecting everyone to everyone does not scale. The frames stay
encrypted end to end, so the server forwards them without being able to
view them.


D29 | Sezione: Features
DOMANDA:
Can I send photos, videos and large files?

RISPOSTA:
Yes. Photos, videos, voice messages, documents, GIFs and stickers all
travel over the same encrypted peer-to-peer channel as text. Large files
are split into chunks and sent in sequence, so an interrupted transfer
resumes from the missing chunks instead of restarting from the beginning.


D30 | Sezione: Features
DOMANDA:
Can I back up my chats in MindTheClub?

RISPOSTA:
Yes. MindTheClub creates an encrypted backup, protected with AES-256-GCM,
which you can restore onto a device. Automatic backup is available as well.
The backup stays under your control rather than being uploaded to a company
cloud, which also means losing both the phone and the backup means losing
the conversations.


D31 | Sezione: Features
DOMANDA:
How do I add a contact on MindTheClub?

RISPOSTA:
Four ways. Scan the other person's QR code face to face, tap an invite link
they have sent you, scan a screenshot of their QR code shared through
another app, or pick someone from your phone's contacts and send them your
profile by MMS. Every method carries a public key fingerprint that your
device verifies before accepting the contact.


SEZIONE 6 - PRICE AND PRACTICAL QUESTIONS


D32 | Sezione: Price and practical questions
DOMANDA:
How much does MindTheClub cost?

RISPOSTA:
MindTheClub costs EUR 1.20 a month after a 30-day free trial. That is the
base price. Google Play converts it into your own currency and adds local
tax where it applies, so the exact amount you will pay is shown on Google
Play before you confirm. There is one tier only and every feature is
included in it.


D33 | Sezione: Price and practical questions
DOMANDA:
Is there a free trial of MindTheClub?

RISPOSTA:
Yes, 30 days. The trial starts when you activate the app rather than when
you download it, so days are not lost to an install you have not opened. No
payment method is requested to begin, and if you do nothing at the end of
the trial the app simply stops.


D34 | Sezione: Price and practical questions
DOMANDA:
How do I cancel my MindTheClub subscription?

RISPOSTA:
The subscription is billed by Google Play, so you cancel it in Play's
subscription settings on your phone, at any time. It then runs until the
end of the period already paid for and does not renew. MindTheClub is not
involved in the billing and cannot charge you separately.


D35 | Sezione: Price and practical questions
DOMANDA:
Is MindTheClub available for iPhone?

RISPOSTA:
No. MindTheClub is Android only. A desktop version is currently in
development, but there is no iOS app and no web version, so today both
people in a conversation need an Android device. This is the app's clearest
limitation.


D36 | Sezione: Price and practical questions
DOMANDA:
Which Android versions does MindTheClub support?

RISPOSTA:
MindTheClub runs on Android 8.0, API level 26, and later. Android 8.0 was
released in 2017, so nearly every phone still in everyday use is covered.
The app is distributed through Google Play, and the store listing confirms
compatibility with your specific device before you install.
